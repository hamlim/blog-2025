This is meant to be an evergreen list of things that leaders should never do, hopefully I __don't__ need to add many to this list!

- Schedule unprompted meetings with no context
  - For sensitive things related to another meeting yet to happen - just wait to schedule the meeting
  - For all other cases, always provide context!
- Send copy-pasted messages as a DM
  - I can tell when the message wasn't written just for me
  - If you intend to share it with all your directs, then post it in a shared channel!
